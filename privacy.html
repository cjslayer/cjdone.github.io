Privacy Policy: claude-gmail-local

Last updated: November 17, 2026

claude-gmail-local is a personal tool written and used only by Christian Done. It signs in to his own Google account and reads his own Gmail messages on his own computer so his personal task system can find the to-do lists he emails himself.

The tool does not collect data from anyone else, is not offered to the public, and does not sell, rent or share any data. Message contents are processed on Christian's computer and by the services he uses to run his own task system. Nothing is used for advertising.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
